Seafarers generator

Or paint by hand: pick a tile, then choose Sea, Off-board (removes the cell), Gold, or any land. "Randomize all" regenerates islands too; "Shuffle resources" keeps the current land shape. Gold fields are picked at ~1 per 10 land tiles. Ports sit on land edges that face the sea.
